# EVERDAWN — The Last Server

> A single-player MMO in your browser. Every other player is an AI. The leaderboard is real.

## Fiction / framing
EVERDAWN was a 2004 fantasy MMO. The official servers closed years ago. One private realm, **Lastlight**,
is still up — and it is full of players who never log off. Every one of them is a bot running the habits of
2006 players: they quest, dance on mailboxes, spam trade chat, ninja loot and stand in fire. You are the only
human online. The realm list says it plainly: `Lastlight — Population: FULL (2,847) · Humans online: 1`.

The fakeness is the feature: the world is bustling, funny and nostalgic, and the one thing that is real — the
global leaderboard and the daily World First race — is shared with every other human who logs in.

## Pillars
1. **It looks like the real thing.** Lush painted valley, big chunky characters, WoW-grade UI. First 30 seconds must sell it.
2. **The server feels alive.** 30–50 SimPlayers with personalities, chat channels, parties, guilds, duels, raid drama.
3. **Combat feels like WoW.** Tab target, GCD, cast bars, procs, floating crits, threat, loot with rarity colors.
4. **Real competition.** Daily dragon (seeded element + affix), World First of the day, fastest kill, parse % with
   Warcraft-Logs colors, speedrun (level 1 → dragon kill), share card + challenge link.

## Session flow
Login screen (realm list joke, daily dragon card, leaderboard) → character creation (race, sex, class, name) →
Everdawn Vale (levels 1–10, ~12 min, quests, SimPlayers) → at level 10 the raid forms → The Ember Maw (10-man raid,
3-phase dragon) → loot rolls → results (time, parse, rank) → share. Title screen also offers **Jump to Raid**
(pre-made level 10) for parse runs.

## Races / classes
- Races (visual + small flavour): Human, Dwarf, Orc, Elf. Sexes: male/female. Plenty of variation for SimPlayers.
- Playable classes: **Warrior** (rage, melee), **Mage** (mana, ranged burst, ground-target AoE, Hot Streak-style proc),
  **Priest** (mana, heals + shadow/holy damage; raid healer role with raid frames).
- SimPlayer visual classes additionally include Rogue, Hunter, Paladin (armor/weapon looks only).

## Everdawn Vale (zone)
Bounded valley (~520 m) ringed by mountains. Ember Peak (the raid) smokes in the north and is visible from anywhere.
- **Dawnhollow** (village, south-centre): inn, smithy, houses, well, square with the World First statue, mailbox.
- **Goldfield Farms** (west): wheat, windmill, pumpkins — Bristleback Boars (1–3).
- **Whisperwood** (east): big oaks, Timber Wolves (2–5), rare elite *Old Greymaw*.
- **Mirrormere Lake** (centre-west): dock, fisherman — Gurglers (fish-men, 3–6).
- **Candlerock Mine** (north-west cliffs): Kobolds with candles (4–7), boss *Chief Waxbeard*.
- **Webwood Hollow** (north-east): Webwood Spiders (5–8).
- **Redcloak Ruins** (south-east): Redcloak Bandits (6–9), elite leader *Vex Redcloak*.
- **Ember Road** (north): switchbacks to the raid portal.

## Quests (1→10)
1. Wolves at the Door (kill 6 Timber Wolves) · 2. Bacon Bits (6 Boar Haunches) · 3. Mail Call (deliver letter to lake)
4. Gurgle Trouble (8 Gurglers) · 5. Not the Candle! (8 kobolds + 5 candles) · 6. Old Greymaw (rare elite)
7. Silk and Venom (6 spider silk) · 8. The Redcloak Menace (8 bandits + Vex) · 9. Into the Ember Maw (reach the portal at 10)

## The raid — The Ember Maw
10-man: player + 9 SimPlayers (2 tanks, 2 healers, DPS). Raid leader calls mechanics in /raid and raid warnings.
Daily dragon: element (Ember / Frost / Venom / Storm / Shadow) + generated name + one affix.
- P1 (100–65%): ground. Cleave (front), Tail Swipe (rear knockback), Breath on tank, elemental ground hazard.
- P2 (65–40%): airborne. Circles, Deep Breath line across the arena (dodge), whelp waves from nests.
- P3 (40–0%): lands enraged. Floor eruptions, raid-wide pulses, Bellowing Roar (short fear). Berserk at 8:00.
Loot: Need/Greed rolls with SimPlayers; epics; ~1% mount (Reins of the <Element> Drake).

## Competition
- Daily seed (UTC date) → dragon + affix + rare spawn locations.
- Boards: World First today, fastest kill, parse % per class (WCL colours: grey<25, green<50, blue<75, purple<95,
  orange<99, pink<100, gold=100), speedrun 1→kill.
- Share card (canvas image) + challenge link. Offline fallback: local bests, synthetic percentile curve.

## Tone
PG, affectionate parody of 2006 MMO culture. Original names only — no Blizzard trademarks.
